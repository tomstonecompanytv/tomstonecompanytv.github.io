Five worlds. Four dragons. One girl who can hear the water.

DRAGON RIDERS is an animated family adventure conceived as a franchise: a hybrid feature film with live-action scenes on Earth and fully animated worlds, followed by a game and a streaming series. Built in Zürich, positioned for a global audience.

Format
Hybrid feature film (live-action / animation / CGI), game, streaming series
